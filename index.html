About Us

ATBUDEVS bridges the gap between classroom theory and production-grade software.

Learn. Build. Impact.

Build production-grade software across Web, Mobile, AI/ML, UI/UX, and Cybersecurity by contributing to live open-source repositories alongside fellow student developers, with direct industry access through high-stakes hackathons, technical bootcamps, and career mentorship.

Membership & Community

Student Membership

Membership is open to every registered student of Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University across all faculties and departments. Gain access to hands-on bootcamps, collaborative software teams, peer mentorship, and verified club credentials with an official Membership Identification Number.

Active Developer Community

Connect with fellow builders through specialized tracks in Web, Mobile, AI, and Cybersecurity. Collaborate on live open-source projects, participate in university hackathons, and attend technical meetups designed to prepare you for real-world engineering careers.

Everything to know before you join

Membership is open to all registered students of Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University across every department, faculty, and level. Whether you are writing your first line of code or already shipping applications, there are no prerequisites to join.

We operate active technical directorates covering Full-Stack Web Development, Mobile App Engineering (iOS/Android), Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, UI/UX Product Design, and Cybersecurity.

Not at all. Club schedules, hackathons, and sessions are specifically built around the university's academic calendar to complement your coursework with real-world engineering rather than compete with it.

Registered members receive an official Membership Identification Number, access to collaborative GitHub repositories, hands-on bootcamps, technical workshops, and mentorship pipelines with industry professionals.

Projects built within the club are maintained as collaborative and open-source software, ensuring student builders receive full moral recognition and portfolio assets for every line of code they ship.
