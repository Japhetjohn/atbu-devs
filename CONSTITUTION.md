ARTICLE I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS
Section 1: Short Title
This document shall be cited as The Constitution of the Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University
Developers Club (ATBUDEVS), Version 1.0.

Section 2: Establishment
There is hereby established a student organization to be known as the Abubakar Tafawa
Balewa University Developers Club (ATBUDEVS).
The Club shall be a non-partisan, non-profit, student-led, educational, innovation-driven, and
technology-focused organization established to promote software development, digital
innovation, research, entrepreneurship, and community service within Abubakar Tafawa
Balewa University and beyond.

Section 3: Legal Status
The Club shall operate in accordance with:
1. This Constitution.
2. The rules and regulations of Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University.
3. All applicable laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
In the event of any conflict between this Constitution and the regulations of the University,
the latter shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

Section 4: Supremacy of the Constitution
This Constitution shall be the supreme governing instrument of the Club.
Every member, officer, committee, programme, project, regulation, and policy of the Club
shall derive its authority from and remain subject to this Constitution.
Any act, decision, regulation, or policy inconsistent with this Constitution shall, to the extent
of its inconsistency, be null and void.

ARTICLE III — MEMBERSHIP
Section 1: Membership
Membership of the Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University Developers Club (ATBUDEVS) shall
be open to every duly registered student of Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University who
subscribes to the ideals, objectives, and provisions of this Constitution.
Membership of the Club shall be voluntary and shall not be denied on the grounds of
ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, language, political opinion, faculty, department, or
socioeconomic background.
No person shall be compelled to become or remain a member of the Club.

Section 2: Categories of Membership
Membership of the Club shall comprise the following categories:
(a) Ordinary Members
An Ordinary Member shall be any duly registered student of the University who has fulfilled
the requirements for membership as prescribed by this Constitution.
Ordinary Members shall enjoy all rights and privileges of membership except where
otherwise provided.
(b) Executive Members
Executive Members shall be members who have been duly elected or appointed into offices
established under this Constitution.
Executive Members shall remain ordinary members for all purposes except in the discharge
of their constitutional responsibilities.
(c) Honorary Members
The Club may confer Honorary Membership upon distinguished individuals who have
rendered exceptional service to technology, education, innovation, the University, or the
Club.
Honorary Membership shall be approved by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the Executive
Council.
Honorary Members shall:
i. Attend meetings by invitation.
ii. Advise the Club.
iii. Participate in programmes.
Honorary Members shall not:
a. Vote.

Constitution.

Section 1: Fundamental Rights of Members
Subject to the provisions of this Constitution and the regulations of Abubakar Tafawa Balewa
University, every duly registered member of the Club shall enjoy the following rights:
i. The right to participate fully in the activities, programmes, and affairs of the Club.
ii. The right to vote in elections, referenda, and other matters requiring the decision of
members, subject to the Electoral Regulations.
iii. The right to contest for any elective office for which the member is constitutionally
qualified.
iv. The right to attend meetings of the General Assembly and to contribute to
deliberations in accordance with the Standing Orders of the Club.
v. The right to equal treatment and equal opportunity without discrimination on the
grounds of faculty, department, ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, language, or
socioeconomic background.
vi. The right to access information relating to the lawful activities, projects, finances,
and decisions of the Club, subject to reasonable confidentiality requirements.
vii. The right to submit petitions, complaints, proposals, recommendations, or
memoranda to the Executive Council.
viii. The right to participate in mentorship programmes, technical training, research
initiatives, hackathons, competitions, and other educational activities organized by the
Club.
ix. The right to receive recognition for outstanding contributions made towards the
growth and development of the Club.
x. The right to enjoy every other privilege lawfully conferred by this Constitution.

Section 2: Freedom of Expression
Every member shall have the right to freely express opinions, ideas, suggestions, and
constructive criticism regarding the affairs of the Club.
Such expression shall be exercised responsibly and shall not include:
a. Defamation.
b. Hate speech.
c. Harassment.
d. Incitement to violence.
e. Discriminatory remarks.



Constitution of ATBUDEVS — Version 1.0

f. False or misleading information intended to undermine the Club or its members.

Section 3: Right to Fair Hearing
No member shall be subjected to disciplinary action unless:
i. The allegations are communicated to the member in writing.
ii. The member is given adequate time to prepare a response.
iii. The member is afforded an opportunity to defend himself or herself before the
appropriate authority.
iv. The decision is reached fairly, impartially, and in accordance with this Constitution.
No member shall be condemned unheard.

Section 4: Right to Privacy
Every member shall have the right to the protection of personal information submitted to
the Club.
The Executive Council shall ensure that membership records and personal data are used
solely for lawful and official purposes and shall not disclose such information without lawful
authority or the consent of the member, except where disclosure is required by law or
University regulations.

iii. Conduct himself or herself in a manner befitting the image of the Club.
iv. Attend meetings and activities regularly.
v. Participate actively in programmes and projects of the Club.
vi. Promote unity, cooperation, and mutual respect.
vii. Protect the assets, property, and reputation of the Club.
viii. Promote ethical conduct in the use of technology.
ix. Respect the rights and dignity of fellow members.
x. Comply with lawful directives issued under this Constitution.

Section 7: Professional Obligations
Recognizing the professional nature of the Club, every member shall:
i. Observe internationally accepted standards of professional conduct in computing and

ii. Respect copyrights, patents, trademarks, software licences, and other forms of

Section 12: Non-Transferability of Rights
The rights and privileges conferred upon a member under this Constitution are personal and
shall not be transferred, assigned, delegated, or exercised by proxy except where expressly
provided by this Constitution.

Section 13: Limitation of Rights
The exercise of the rights guaranteed under this Constitution shall be subject to:
i. The provisions of this Constitution.
ii. The lawful decisions of the General Assembly.
iii. The lawful decisions of the Executive Council.
iv. The regulations of Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University.
v. The laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
No provision of this Constitution shall be interpreted in a manner that permits the abuse of
rights or the infringement of the rights of other members.

ARTICLE V — ORGANS OF THE CLUB
Section 1: Establishment of the Organs
For the purpose of ensuring effective administration, democratic governance, accountability,
and the realization of the objectives of the Club, there are hereby established the following
organs of the Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University Developers Club (ATBUDEVS):
i. The General Assembly.
ii. The Executive Council.
iii. Standing Committees.
iv. Ad-hoc Committees.
Each organ shall derive its authority from this Constitution and shall exercise only such
powers as are expressly or impliedly conferred upon it herein.

Section 2: Supremacy of the General Assembly
The General Assembly shall be the supreme deliberative and policy-making organ of the
Club.
Subject to the regulations of Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University, every organ, officer,
committee, or member of the Club shall be accountable to the General Assembly.
No decision of the Executive Council or any Committee shall override a lawful resolution of
the General Assembly.

Section 3: Relationship Among the Organs
The organs of the Club shall operate on the principles of:
a. Cooperation.
b. Mutual respect.
c. Accountability.
d. Transparency.
e. Collective responsibility.
No organ shall unlawfully interfere with the constitutional functions of another.

Section 4: Delegation of Authority
Any organ established under this Constitution may delegate administrative responsibilities
to another organ, committee, or officer.
Provided that:
i. Such delegation shall not amount to an abdication of constitutional responsibility.
ii. The delegating organ shall remain accountable for the exercise of delegated powers.



Constitution of ATBUDEVS — Version 1.0

iii. Delegated authority may be withdrawn where it is abused or improperly exercised.

Every organ of the Club shall, in the discharge of its responsibilities, be guided by the

iii. Transparency.
iv. Accountability.
v. Inclusiveness.
vi. Professionalism.
vii. Merit.

No Committee shall exercise powers beyond those delegated to it.

Every organ of the Club shall maintain proper records to ensure continuity of administration.

equipment, and other official property to their successors not later than fourteen (14) days
after leaving office.
Failure to comply with this provision shall constitute misconduct.

Every organ shall safeguard the reputation, independence, and credibility of the Club.

i. Bringing the Club into disrepute.
ii. Undermining its constitutional authority.

SCHEDULE XIII — TRANSFER OF AUTHORITY (HANDOVER)
TRANSFER OF AUTHORITY (HANDOVER)

Section 1: Handover Period
Every outgoing Executive Officer shall hand over to the incoming Executive Officer within
fourteen (14) days after inauguration.

Section 2: Handover Documents
Every outgoing Executive Officer shall submit:
i. A detailed Handover Note.
ii. Financial records (where applicable).
iii. Project reports.
iv. Official files.
v. Passwords and login credentials.
vi. Hardware and equipment.
vii. Contacts of partners and sponsors.
viii. Recommendations for the incoming administration.

Section 3: Failure to Handover
Failure to comply with this Appendix shall constitute misconduct under this Constitution.

FINAL DECLARATION
WE, the members of the Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University Developers Club (ATBUDEVS),
Recognizing the transformative power of technology;
Inspired by the pursuit of knowledge, innovation, and excellence;
Committed to ethical leadership, collaboration, and service;
Determined to build a vibrant community of developers, designers, innovators, researchers,
and technology enthusiasts;
Resolved to promote learning, creativity, entrepreneurship, and digital inclusion within
Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University and beyond;
Do hereby adopt, enact, and give to ourselves this Constitution as the supreme governing
document of the Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University Developers Club (ATBUDEVS).
May this Constitution guide our decisions, strengthen our unity, preserve our values, and
inspire generations of developers to Code. Create. Impact.
